Welcome to Meadowbrook Rehabilitation

Whether a short-term post hospital rehabilitation patient or a long-term resident of a Meadowbrook Healthcare Resort, you will be treated like family. Our healthcare resorts strive to provide an exceptional level of clinical care and rehabilitation, coupled with amenties that promote healing and comfort. We want nothing more than for our patients and residents to feel at ease whether they are with us for short term rehabilitation or have chosen to make Meadowbrook their home.

 

Our philosophy is simple: Human dignity and personal pride do not diminish with illness, injury or age, but continue throughout the life cycle. Recognition of this, in each and every individual, is our priority. Our goal is to create and provide a supportive and comfortable environment in which each person is motivated to regain and/or retain their maximum level of functioning, both physically and mentally, with continuing dedication, concern and care for the utmost quality of life.

JCAHO accredited in Rehabilitation and Advanced Care
